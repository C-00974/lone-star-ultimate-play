What improved

The front sits lower and the hood rises more coherently into the fenders. Local vent ramps add shape. The bumper transition, lamp-pocket fit and side marker fit are repaired. The side-view lamp protrusions are resolved.

What remains

The far lamp housing is too angular and its projector lenses lack realistic optical depth. Fascia corners and side ducts remain too upright; the lower return is flat. Existing greenhouse seams and cockpit details remain approximate.

Next priorities: lamp outlines and optics, then fascia corner and duct contours.

Try it in the game

Choose “Take a drive · Crew stable,” then press E beside the Evora to enter. Press V to cycle cameras; the third view change reaches the cockpit. Use W/S to drive and brake, A/D to steer, Space for the handbrake, L for headlights and E to exit when stopped.

The exact build completed normal entry, cockpit, live gauges, headlights, forward driving, braking and exit checks. Physics, driver eye and control behavior were preserved.
